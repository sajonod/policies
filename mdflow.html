Privacy Policy for MD Flow Markdown Viewer and Editor

Effective Date May 07, 2026

MD Flow ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information Collection and Use

mdflow is a simple file viewer and editor for Markdown (.md) files. Our primary goal is to provide a local-first utility.

  • Local Files: Our app requires access to your device's storage to open, read, and save .md files. We do not upload your files to any external servers. All editing and viewing happen locally on your device.
  • Permissions: We request permission to access your device's storage/files via the File Picker plugin to enable the core functionality of the app.

2. Third-Party Services

To support the app and provide certain features, we use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you.

  • Google Play Services: Used for app distribution and core Android functionality.
  • Google AdMob: We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect data such as your Device ID, location (if enabled), and interaction with ads to provide personalized advertising.
    Google’s Privacy & Terms

3. Log Data

In the case of an error in the app, we may collect data and information (through third-party products) on your phone called Log Data. This may include information such as your device Internet Protocol (“IP”) address, device name, operating system version, the configuration of the app when utilizing our service, and other statistics.

4. Security

We value your trust in providing us your information, but remember that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable. Since mdflow operates primarily offline for file editing, the risk is minimized.

5. Children’s Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at the email provided in the PlayStore App information section.
