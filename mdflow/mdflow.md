
***Privacy Policy for mdflow***


**Effective Date: May 07, 2026]**

mdflow ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose information when you use our mobile application.


**1. Information Collection and Use**
mdflow is a simple file viewer and editor for Markdown (.md) files.
•
Local Files: Our app requires access to your device's storage to open, read, and save .md files. We do not upload your files to any external servers. All editing and viewing happen locally on your device.
•
Permissions: We may request permission to access your device's storage/files via the File Picker plugin to enable the core functionality of the app.

**4. Security**
We value your trust in providing us your information, but remember that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable. Since mdflow operates primarily offline for file editing, the risk is minimized.
